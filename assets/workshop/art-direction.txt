WORKSHOP / SERVICE BAY 03

Baseline: 4472f2d on game/runtime. Working branch: codex/workshop-3d.
Underground vaulted basalt and red-clay brick bay. Riveted navy iron ribs, burnished aged
brass, oxidized copper piping, neutral service lamps over the hero tank, warm
wall lamps and a restrained cool backlight. Real kit meshes are authoritative.
Precision lift and robot remain parametric, with authored static surroundings.

materials-atlas.png was generated with the built-in image generation tool.
Prompt: Production albedo texture atlas for a realistically CAD-like steampunk
underground tank workshop rendered in a stylized physically based game engine.
Square 2x2 material sample atlas, flat orthographic surfaces, no perspective,
lighting gradients or shadows. Top left: grey blue excavated basalt with subtle
veins. Top right: aged dark navy gunmetal plate, fine scratches and oil wear.
Bottom left: aged golden brass with gentle patina. Bottom right: oxidized copper,
warm reddish brown and sparse verdigris. Four equal square quadrants entirely
filled with materials. No text, objects, bolts, gaps, borders, or watermarks.
Restrained physically plausible wear. Bake this image onto actual 3D geometry.

Blender 5.2.1 LTS was verified through MCP. The room is authored and exported
with a separate headless Blender process so the user's interactive Blender can
remain closed. scripts/blender/build-workshop.py is the reproducible source.

Meshy 7.1 generated the boiler, lathe, rolling cabinets, inert ordnance cart,
twin-cylinder compressor and reinforced oak parts crates
using the image references in meshy/. The dynamic fan rotor uses the same
original-geometry pipeline. All original meshes and PBR maps are preserved
(4K base color and normal, 2K metallic/roughness).
Job receipts and generation provenance are recorded under meshy/. The official
MCP verified the balance; the official REST API explicitly requested model 7.1
because the installed MCP schema did not expose that model literal.

Walls use individual .48m x .20m red-clay bricks, alternating running bond and
recessed 12mm mortar joints. The elliptical vault uses fitted stone voussoirs.
The rear fan has a real 1.48m-radius liner, with a clear 1.55m masonry opening,
and a twelve-metre receding open duct bending upward at z=-20.1, plus a
separate original-geometry rotor for runtime rotation. The pressure gauge keeps
its original bezel and replaces only the dial face with an independent image
and moving pointer; its measured plane is recorded in manifest.pressureGauge.

workshop.blend links the exact original textures in texture-source/ and
stores no packed texture duplicates. It can be reopened and rendered directly.
The two cabinet instances share one texture layer; their bolted adapter frames
end at the mechanical payload support datum y=.75m. No prop is decimated.
The seven jobs consumed 245 of the authorized 450 Meshy credits.

Architecture uses dedicated world-projected image textures: red clay brick
(material15,.4m repeat), old cold concrete (16,1.5m repeat) and matte rough stone
voussoirs (17,.6m repeat). Geometry supplies real bond and narrow mortar joints;
the images supply grain, pits and wear. The floor is one continuous poured slab.
Every procedural pipe elbow is a tangent circular sweep with oriented flange
collars and visible bolts. The fan cowling is one coherent non-overlapping
annular casting; receding duct stiffeners and a supported drive shaft give depth.
Millimetre relief on exposed brick and stone faces supplements the bitmap bump;
all mortar edges and masonry bed dimensions remain fixed. Static brass uses
the same authored rest-space material19 bitmap as the runtime service hardware.
The freight gate uses captured end rollers, nineteen interlocking formed slats,
a supported winding drum and geared motor, manual override and an operator box.
Its central approach lane remains clear. The pit is exactly3.44m x4.20m with
bottom y=-.72; fixed cover plates end25mm outside the2.85m x3.90m moving deck.
Return cabinet is[-3,0,-3.6], delivery[-4.55,0,1.2]. The shelf/crates moved rearward
toz=-2.7 and the ordnance cart forward toz=5.0 to clear their enlarged service beds.

reflection-probe/ contains six scene-linear EXRs rendered at [0,1.7,0] from
the actual static room. public/workshop/reflection-*.webp encode RGBM range32
without a tone-map or gamma transform. The engine decodes HDR before GGX
prefiltering and projects reflection directions into the room's physical box.
No tank, lift, robot or animated fan was baked into the probe. The reproducible
script is scripts/blender/render-workshop-probe.py; metadata includes exact
cube orientation and box bounds in assets/workshop/runtime-source/reflection-probe.json.

Shipping texture packaging: scripts/workshop/pack-workshop-textures.py keeps
unaltered original maps and SHA256s in texture-source/, then writes2K albedo
and vector-renormalized2K normals, plus1K variance-filtered roughness/metallic.
Color uses WebP quality90. The --compact pack writes X/Y normal components as
separate grayscale planes at quality94, retaining exact Z-sign alpha. The GPU
reconstructs the ordinary2K normal array once at load time; all seven maps have
p95 angular error below1.89 degrees after RGBA8 quantization. Roughness and
metallic use six-bit scalar quantization, maximum error2/255, then lossless WebP.
Architecture/fixture maps use quality94 and the gauge quality96 at native size.
RGBM remains native-size lossless RGBA, checked byte-for-byte including alpha.
The full geometry plus compact textures totals about40MB; every original map,
source GLB and geometry triangle is preserved in the source asset directories.
Measured codec alternatives and final error bounds are in texture-source/.
Reproduction order: build-workshop.py, render-workshop-probe.py, texture packer,
then runtime geometry packaging. Do not rerun Blender export over a packaged
runtime without rerunning both packaging steps.

The parked static overhead crane has flange-riding runway wheels, transverse
hoist trolley, outer bolted carrier plates, stub axles and motor reduction.
Its two chain falls form a continuous reeving path around a supported sheave;
a thrust swivel, retained clevis pin and gated tapered hook complete the load
path. This architectural crane is separate from the animated tank service rig.
The machinist's tabouret at [4.58,0,1.22] has a dished worn steel seat at y=.854,
a genuine coarse trapezoidal spindle, captive bronze nut and locking lever,
supporting cast hub, three splayed tubular legs, floor-flush pads and a connected
footrest. Its screw shoulder bears against the underside of the seat boss.

target-scene.png is generated concept art, not an in-game screenshot. Its prompt
is saved in target-scene-prompt.txt. The user approved its functional layout and
lighting direction, but requested a cleaner, more gamified finish compatible
with the existing tanks. Keep grime localized, service aisles clear, all lamps
outside the crane's swept volume, and drains recessed and purposeful. No large
brand signs. The interface is called Workshop, with contextual controls and
compact overall stats rather than permanently occluding panels.
