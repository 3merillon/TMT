Tiny Metal Tank campaign speech

Generated offline with Kokoro-82M (hexgrad), using onnx-community's timestamped
fp32 ONNX conversion at dd4401a9add81ac692d20e240d22ec9dda82cc29.
Model and voice packs: Apache-2.0; license text accompanies this directory.
https://huggingface.co/onnx-community/Kokoro-82M-v1.0-ONNX-timestamped
https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M

Uses the Planets/Pinionfall offline speech pipeline, HeadTTS and Misaki English
phonemisation. Their retained licenses accompany this directory. Model weights,
style vectors, tokenizer, and inference runtime are not shipped in this game.
Character casting, prosody, EQ, coloration, mastering and Opus compression are
recorded in scripts/bake-campaign-voice.ts and manifest.json.
These are synthetic performances, not recordings or imitations of named actors.

Upstream model-card acknowledgements: StyleTTS 2 (Li et al.), trainer @rzvzn,
Koniwa tnc (CC BY 3.0) and SIWIS (CC BY 4.0). Those datasets are not included.
